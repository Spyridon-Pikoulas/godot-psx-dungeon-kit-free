# PSX Dungeon Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build a dungeon cell** for your dark fantasy game: flagstone and straw floors, a stone ceiling, dark stone and rubble walls, an arched doorway and its oak door, the cell's bars and their door, a corner post, a torch that burns, a banner that stirs, a chest that opens, a straw bed and the last prisoner, still chained to the wall. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a flagstone floor and one of straw, a stone ceiling, a dark stone wall and a
  rubble one, the arched doorway and a corner post, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **Doors:** an oak door strapped with iron and the cell door in its bars, both swinging open, their
  colliders moving with them.
- **The cell:** a straw bed, a skeleton chained to the wall, a chest that opens.
- **Fittings:** a wall torch whose fire flickers, the house's red banner, stirring.
- **5 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- One 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (a doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your torches fall on it, and a
gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the piece in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
